Our Inventory

Here, take this tools from me. It's a quick-swap tools, you can access any item in your inventory quickly with this, but you have to know their location in your inventory. Long ago, people used to call their inventory a list because they store everything in order from 0 up to their inventory size - 1. Even a great coder can't remember every location of their item in the list, but we always know what item we need to fight against something. Hence, the great coders created a unique technology in the past called dictionary, it can store anything in pairs. With this, you can store the location and item name of each item that you pick up along the journey.

Village 3 • List & Dictionary

Our Inventory

Let's say you encounter various monsters during your journey and you have a dictionary of weaknesses of monsters, so you just need to swap to the right tool to fight them. How will you do this? You never know which monster will come next, so you can’t prepare the tool in advance.

Task

Define a function quick_look_swap(monster, weaknesses, inventory) that takes a monster name, a dictionary of monster-to-weapon pairs weaknesses, and a list of items in your inventory.

• Use the weaknesses dictionary to look up the counter-weapon for the given monster.

• If the required weapon is inside your inventory list, return its index.

• If the weapon is not in your inventory (or the monster is not in the dictionary), return -1.

Examples

quick_look_swap('harpy', weaknesses, inventory) # Returns: '2' quick_look_swap('bandits', weaknesses, inventory) # Returns: '5'

Guidelines

Create a dictionary with curly braces: example_dictionary = {'fruit': 'banana', 'metal': 'iron', 'animal': 'dog'}.

Access a dictionary value using brackets: example_of_fruit = dictionary['fruit'].

Get item position with list_name.index(item).
