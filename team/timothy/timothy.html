// About me

My name is Timothy Pardin, I'm an Indonesian student in my second year of Artificial Intelligence at Beijing Institute of Technology. I moved here in 2025 and I'm still not used to the winter.

My interests sits across software engineering, data management, and human-centric psychology. What connects them for me is behavioural research also studying how people actually think and work, then using that to shape how a system talks to them. That's what pulled me toward context engineering. I still have a lot to learn in all three, and that's mostly the point.

// My role

In our team, Potato Duck, I'm the Project Manager/CEO. I wrote the project plan the whole team such as the task list, the schedule, the risks, and who owns what. During the build I run our meetings, track progress, and triage the bugs Steave finds. At the end I put together the project logs, the final report, and the submission.

I'm not only on the planning side. I set up the repository and folder structure with Andrew, and I build the shared page templates, navigation and responsive CSS together with him and Cornelius, so every page on the site starts from the same skeleton. I also write the hint engine that nudges learners when they're stuck, package the site for Android, and help fix defects once all the pieces come together.

I've done this before as a Scrum project manager during an internship in Jakarta, and as team lead on a small game called Aisling.
