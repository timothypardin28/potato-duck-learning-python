Village 5 • Time and Memory

Your Consideration

Think about this, you want to fuse two crystal power into one stronger crystal. However, two distinct crystals must have their crystal power summed up to a specific target power needed, not more or less. You have gotten tons of crystals in your inventory, how should you solve this problem efficiently without wasting your mana and time?

Task

Define a function fuse_mana_crystals(crystals, target_power) that takes a list of integers crystals and an integer target_power.

Your function must determine if there are two distinct crystals in the list whose combined powers equal target_power. Return True if such a pair exists, or False otherwise.

Spell Constraints:

Time Limit (Duration): Maximum 0.50 seconds. A quadratic algorithm like nested loops or repeated linear searches inside a loop will take too long and trigger a Time Limit Exceeded error!

Mana Limit (Memory): Maximum 3.5 MB. Do not allocate massive lists of all pair combinations or large matrices, or your duck body will suffer a Mana Overload!

Examples

# Two distinct crystals (10 + 7 = 17) fuse_mana_crystals([10, 15, 3, 7], 17) # Returns: True # No two crystals can form 20 fuse_mana_crystals([10, 15, 3, 7], 20) # Returns: False

Guidelines

• Why Brute Force Fails: Checking every pair using two nested for loops takes O(n2) time. On thousands of crystals, this requires millions of operations and exceeds your time limit.

• Conserving Mana: Pre-generating every possible crystal combination takes excessive memory and drains all your mana.

• The Optimal Spell: As you inspect each crystal once, calculate what power you still need. If the needed crystal power is already in your checked set, you immediately found a valid pair! Otherwise, add the current crystal to checked set.

• In Python, looking up an element in a set() takes constant time O(1), which lets you solve the entire problem in a single pass $O(N)$ with minimal memory.
