Guardian 9 • Time Efficiency

The Hungry Cobra

Because you are pinned, you can only reach in with one hand to grab max 3 continuous bottles at a time. Quick! gives the Cobra the maximum total energy before it eats you!

Task

Define a function grabBottle(inv_energyVal, nBottle) that accepts a list inv_energyVal which contain list inverted energy, e.g. the energy of 97 is 79.

Your function should return the sum of value of nBottle integers that have the largest real energy value, not inverted energy value

Examples

grabBottle([21, 90, 19, 46, 50, 1, 73], 3) # Returns: '192'

Guidelines

Think how to eliminate non potential number to make the number of element to search become smaller, so you don't have to flip all numbers.
