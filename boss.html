The Pythorn

Quackbit finally reach the PyThorn's Nest, PyThorn is waiting in its nest to defeat Quackbit. PyThorn body is 25 times bigger than Quackbit, Quackbit doesn't stand a chance if he face PyThorn directly. He has to look for a new strategy.

Boss

The Pythorn

There is a scar on the PyThorn's body, it must be the weak spot of PyThorn. Quackbit is planning to stab on the middle point of that scar because Quackbit is afraid he will miss if he targets the edge point. Based on his observation towards PyThorn's movement, Quackbit can perform at most two times attack safely. He just needs to think when is the best time to attack PyThorn.

Task

Define a function choose_stabbingPoint(x, y) that accepts two list of integers x and y which are the coordinate in PyThorn body. Your function should return the median of these sorted list in O(log(m+n)) runtime complexity. PyThorn wont wait for you while you were thinking where to stab it, you should choose the point quickly.


You also need to define a function maxDamage(weak_degree) that accepts a list of integers weak_degree. Your function should return the maximal damage you can deal with at most two stabs, that maximal damage is largest difference of weak degree you can make in one stab (endWeakness - initialWeakness) and you can't perform two stabs simultaneously.


If you're able to choose the right stabbing point and correct timing to attack, you can continue this tempo of fighting and defeat PyThorn.

Examples

choose_stabbingPoint([1, 2], [3, 4]) # Returns: 2.500 maxDamage([3, 3, 5, 0, 0, 3, 1, 4]) # Returns: 6 Explanation The median of [1, 2, 3, 4] is 2.5 Stab on t = 4 (weakness = 0) and pull out on t = 6 (weakness = 3), damage = 3 - 0 = 3. Then stab on t = 7 (weakness = 1) and pull out on t = 8 (weakness = 4), damage = 4 - 1 = 3.
