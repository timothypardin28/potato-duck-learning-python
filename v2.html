Something Redundant

Imagine this scenario, quackbit. You are trapped in the middle of a magical cave created by Pythorn. There are a lot of monsters chasing you and you definitely don't want to look away from them when they're chasing, so you need to keep checking is there any monster chasing. However, here is the catch, this cave has a curse for anyone that looks back for more than 3 times, that person will be trapped forever in the cave. Therefore, I think ensuring no monster is chasing you for 3 times is enough to make decision to keep walking until you're out from the cave. How should the quackbit act to get out from the cave safely?

Task

Define a function be_careful(distance) that take a distance and make the quackbit looks back three times before keep walking out of the magical cave.

The keywords are 'look back' and 'walk'

Guidelines

You can make a loop using for statement and use print() to print text on your terminal.
